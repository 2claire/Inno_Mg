What We Expect

Our Supplier Standards.

Every supplier working with MG Innovation Hub is expected to uphold these standards — no exceptions. They protect our community, your reputation, and the quality of everything we build together.

01
✅

Legal Compliance

All suppliers must hold valid KRA PIN, Tax Compliance Certificate and business registration documents. Compliance with Kenyan labour law is mandatory.

02
🤝

Integrity & Ethics

Zero tolerance for bribery, corruption or fraudulent practices. Suppliers must sign and uphold the MG Supplier Code of Conduct before any contract is awarded.

03
🏆

Quality of Delivery

Products and services must meet agreed specifications. We operate SLA-based contracts — consistent underperformance results in offboarding with written notice.

04
⏱️

Timeliness

On-time delivery is a core KPI for all supplier relationships. Delays must be communicated at least 48 hours in advance with a revised delivery plan.

05
🌍

Local & Sustainable

We prioritise Nakuru-based and Kenyan-owned businesses. Suppliers are encouraged to adopt environmentally responsible practices in their operations.

06
🔒

Data & Confidentiality

Suppliers must safeguard all MG Innovation Hub data they access. NDAs are required for any supplier with access to member, financial or operational information.

Supplier FAQ

Questions Answered.

Who can apply to become a supplier?
+
Any registered business in Kenya can apply. We prioritise Nakuru-based and Kenyan-owned businesses, but welcome applications from across East Africa for specialised services not locally available. You must hold valid KRA PIN and business registration documents.
Is there a fee to apply or to be listed?
+
No — the application process and supplier directory listing are completely free. We do not charge any onboarding, registration or listing fees. Revenue from our supplier relationships comes only from actual contracts awarded.
How long does the vetting process take?
+
Initial application review takes up to 5 business days. Full due diligence — including document verification, reference checks, and an optional site visit — takes up to 10 additional business days. You will receive a written response either way.
What documents do I need to submit?
+
Download the Supplier Application Form above and complete it in full. You will also need to attach: Certificate of Incorporation or Business Name Registration, KRA PIN Certificate, Tax Compliance Certificate, company profile or capability statement, and at least two reference contacts from previous clients.
What are the payment terms?
+
All verified supplier invoices are paid within 14 calendar days of receipt and approval — no exceptions. We do not operate 30-day or 60-day payment cycles. Submit your invoice with correct bank details and our finance team will process it promptly.
Can MG alumni startups become suppliers?
+
Absolutely — and we actively encourage it. MG alumni startups that offer relevant products or services are fast-tracked through the supplier application process. This is a core part of our commitment to circulating value within the Nakuru innovation ecosystem.
What happens after the pilot period?
+
Approved suppliers begin with a 3-month pilot contract with clear SLAs. If performance meets our benchmarks and community feedback is positive, you receive full Verified Supplier status, a preferred vendor agreement, and a promoted listing in our directory. Suppliers who underperform are offboarded with written feedback.
