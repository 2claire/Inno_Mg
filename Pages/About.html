Who We Are

Building Africa's
Innovation Backbone.

MG Innovation Hub was founded in 2022 by entrepreneurs, and investors who saw a gap: East Africa had world-class talent but lacked a home for it. So they built one.

Today we operate from Nakuru Riva Plaza CBD, running incubation programs, workshops and investment networks that have collectively helped 6+ startups reach product-market fit, raise capital, and scale across the continent.

Mission

Accelerate Africa's most impactful innovators from idea to scale.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values.

These aren't framed posters on a wall. They're the operating principles that shape every program, every partnership, and every conversation.

⚡

Audacious Ambition

We don't celebrate incremental. We back founders who want to redefine industries, not just optimise them. Big swings are the norm here.

🤝

Community First

The hub is built on reciprocity. Every member contributes knowledge, connections, and energy — and receives the same in return. Isolation ends at our doors.

🌍

Africa-First Lens

Every solution we build must make sense in context. We design for African infrastructure, African users, and African opportunity — not imported frameworks.

🔬

Evidence Over Hype

We ask hard questions. We demand data. Enthusiasm is welcome but it doesn't replace rigorous testing, user research, and honest validation.

🔓

Radical Transparency

With our founders, our partners, and our community — we say what we mean. Clear feedback, honest guidance, and no hidden agendas.

🚀

Execution Wins

Ideas are cheap. Velocity is rare. We celebrate founders who ship fast, learn fast, and iterate relentlessly — momentum over perfection, always.
